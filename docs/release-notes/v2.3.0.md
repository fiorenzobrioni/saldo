# 💶 Saldo 2.3.0

**More answers from the same data, and a look shared with Chiaro and Passo.** An account now
has its own page, a recurring transaction can be paused, a bank's CSV can be mapped by hand,
and widgets, notifications and the icon are new. No account, no ads, no tracking, as before.

## 📦 Download and install

| | |
|---|---|
| File | `saldo-v2.3.0.apk`, with `saldo-v2.3.0.apk.sha256` beside it |
| Requires | Android 13 (API 33) or newer |
| Signature | the project's release key, as in 2.2.0 |

- **From 2.2.0**: install it over the old one. Your data stays, and the database is updated on
  the first start.
- **From 2.1.0 or earlier**: export a backup, uninstall, install 2.3.0 and restore the backup
  on the first run (the signing key changed in 2.2.0).
- **Check the download**: `sha256sum -c saldo-v2.3.0.apk.sha256`, and compare the signing
  certificate with the fingerprint in the [README](../../README.md#install).

## ✨ New

- 🏦 **Account details**: tap an account for its balance over 30 days, its card, loan or
  savings goal, its transactions month by month, and every action on it.
- 📄 **Duplicate a transaction**: from the editor or with a long press on a row, dated now.
- ⏸️ **Pause a recurring transaction**: it stops charging and counting, and resumes from
  today without catching up.
- 🔔 **Backup reminder**, optional: every 7, 14 or 30 days since your last backup.
- 📥 **CSV column mapping**: when a bank's file is not recognised, map its columns by hand
  and save the mapping for the next file.
- 🗓️ **Every four months**, a new frequency for recurring transactions, and eight new
  category icons (utilities, insurance, transport pass, newspapers, medicines).
- 📖 **The guide**, in the app: the four screens, and what a screen cannot say on its own.

## 🎨 Changed

- 🏠 **Widgets**: the card of Chiaro and Passo, light, dark, the phone's or one of six colours,
  any opacity, with a live preview in their settings.
- 🔔 **Notifications**: one sentence with the number that matters, the details when you open
  it, silent between 22:00 and 7:00.
- 💶 **A new app icon**: a ring of income and expenses, with a coin.

## 🔧 Fixed

- **The month stops at today**: a transaction dated later this month no longer counts in the
  Dashboard's month before its day (it still counts as committed in Safe to spend).
- **Card statements count manual payments**: a transfer to the card before the due date
  lowers the amount charged, and a statement closed in credit lowers the next one.
- **Statistics** for the current month and year stop at today, and categories used for both
  expenses and income are counted correctly.
- **CSV import** reads amounts like `1,234` by the file's own convention, not by guess.

The full list of features is in the [README](../../README.md); the user guide (in Italian)
explains each one: [docs/guida-utente/](../guida-utente/README.md).
